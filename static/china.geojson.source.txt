Source: DataV GeoAtlas China administrative boundary GeoJSON
Base URL: https://geo.datav.aliyun.com/areas_v3/bound
Reference: https://geojson.cn/data/atlas/china

Files:
- static/china-provinces.geojson: generated from 100000_full.json, used for the map outline, chunk clipping, province boundary lines, and province labels.
- static/china-cities.geojson: generated by merging province-level child data from {adcode}_full.json, used for city-level boundary lines and Chinese city labels.

Generation notes:
- Regular provinces use child features whose properties.level is "city".
- Direct-controlled municipalities and regions without city-level child features fall back to their own province-level geometry as one city-display feature.
- Features without a Chinese properties.name are filtered out.
- The generated static files are committed so the frontend does not depend on external network access at runtime.
